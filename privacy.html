Shrtlist Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · ApogeeLab

Summary

Shrtlist keeps your information on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no servers run by ApogeeLab. We never receive, sell, or share your data.

The app does connect to other companies' websites for you: employers' career sites and the hiring systems they use. Searches send them only search terms. When you apply, the information you choose to fill in and submit goes to that employer, and their own privacy policy covers it. The section What leaves your phone lists exactly what goes where.

This policy covers the Shrtlist Android app. In this policy, "we" means ApogeeLab and "you" means the person using the app.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, and we never receive it.

How the app uses the internet

When you run a search, the app downloads public job listings straight from employers' career sites and their hiring-system providers (such as Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, and Workday). These requests go directly from your phone to those sites, not through us.

The requests contain search terms such as job titles and locations. They do not contain your name, contact details, resume, or answers. Like any website visit, those sites can see your IP address and basic device information, under their own privacy policies.

Applying to jobs and autofill

Autofill only puts your saved answers into fields you tap yourself, and it never submits anything for you. You review every page and decide whether to send it.

  • Application forms open in the app's built-in browser, loaded directly from the employer's site.
  • Tapping a field fills it from your answer library. Tapping a resume upload attaches your resume.
  • Autofill never fills voluntary self-identification questions (such as gender, race, veteran, or disability status) or "I agree" and "I certify" boxes.
  • A Pause button turns autofill off at any time.

Information you submit on an application goes to that employer and is handled under the employer's privacy policy, not this one.

Saved passwords for career sites

If you choose to, Shrtlist saves the passwords you use on employer career sites so it can sign you in, and it can create a strong password when you make a new account. These passwords never leave your phone.

  • Encrypted on the phone. Each password is encrypted with a key that is created inside your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore) and cannot be copied off the device.
  • Locked behind your screen lock. Using a saved password requires your fingerprint, face, or screen-lock PIN, after which they stay unlocked for 5 minutes.
  • Only for the right site. A password is filled only on the exact website it was saved for, which protects against look-alike sites.
  • Never shared. Saved passwords are not synced to another device, not sent to any server (including ours), and not included in backups. We cannot see or recover them.

You can instead leave password fields to your phone's own password manager, and you can view, edit, or delete saved passwords at any time in Settings › Saved passwords. If you remove your phone's screen lock, Android permanently disables the key, and the saved passwords can no longer be opened.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your personal information or run servers that receive it.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. (Information you submit to an employer goes from your phone to that employer, at your direction, not through us.)
  • We don't show ads or include advertising or analytics tools.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't require an account or sign-in.

Permissions, security, and backups

The app asks for three permissions: internet access, which it uses to download job listings and load application pages; notifications, to tell you about new matches from automatic searches (only if you allow them); and fingerprint or face unlock, which it uses only to unlock your saved passwords. It cannot reach your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files. The only exception is a file you pick yourself in the system file picker, such as your resume.

Your data is kept in storage that only this app can read. The app opts out of Android cloud backup, so its data is not copied to Google's backup service. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app, so keep a screen lock on.

Your choices and deleting your data

Your data stays until you remove it. Because we hold none of it, you can delete all of it yourself, with no request to us.

  • Edit or clear answers and settings: use the app's Settings screen, including Settings › Saved passwords.
  • Delete everything on the phone: clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Children, changes, and contact

Children. The app is meant for job seekers and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the app sends nothing to us.

Users outside the US. The app works the same everywhere. Your data stays on your phone, and it goes only to the career sites you search and apply on.

Changes. If this policy changes, we will post the new version with a new effective date and note the change in the app's release notes.
